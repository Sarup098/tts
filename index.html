About Me

Hey Amigos 👋

My name is Tashi Tseten Lepcha, son of Sonam Pintso Lepcha and Chuden Lepcha, and I have a sibling named Tseten Tashi Lepcha.

I come from Sikkim, from a small and beautiful village called Lum, nestled quietly in the valleys of Dzongu—a place that has shaped my roots, my values, and my way of seeing the world.

I am a student, currently pursuing a Master of Science in Physics. Alongside science, I’ve always carried a deep curiosity for history. I’ve been fascinated by stories that time has slowly buried—truths left unspoken, memories covered in dust, waiting to be rediscovered.

That curiosity is what led me to begin this podcast journey. I genuinely enjoy listening to people—well, not everyone 😄—but those whose words carry meaning, experience, and value. Conversations like these inspire me, teach me, and remind me why stories matter.

Thank you for being here and for sharing a part of this journey with me.

Much love 🤍
