About Me

Tashi Tseten Lepcha

Podcaster • Science Student • Storyteller from Dzongu

I’m Tashi Tseten Lepcha, a podcaster and content creator from Lum, Dzongu, with a deep respect for stories, culture, and ideas that shape who we are. Through my work, I try to create thoughtful conversations—especially around history, identity, and lived experiences—while learning from the people and voices I encounter along the way.

I’m grateful to have a growing community of listeners and viewers, with 3.2K+ subscribers on YouTube, 16K followers on Facebook, and 3K on Instagram. Their support keeps me motivated to listen more carefully and share more responsibly.
